How to print on the Zebra
  1. Tap Save and choose One combined file. In the share sheet, choose Save to Files, then tap the .zip in Files to unpack a real .zpl file.
  2. USB drive: save it to a flash drive connected to your iPhone, plug the drive into the ZT411, and go to Storage → Print from USB on the touchscreen.
  3. Computer: Zebra Setup Utilities → Open Communication with Printer → Send File. On a Mac: lpr -P <printer> -o raw labels.zpl
  4. Don't send it through AirPrint or a normal print dialog. That prints the code as text and shrinks labels.
